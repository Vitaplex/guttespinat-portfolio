## Acting Out Of Thought/Emotion
A key point in developing a more grounded mindset, is to realize - and know, even in the worst moments - that **your thoughts and your feelings are not you, and do not define you!**
* Anger is not you 
* Anxiety is not you
* Fear is not you
* Jealousy is not you
* Happiness is not you

Your emotions are valid, and should be listened to and acted upon. It is, however, when the emotions take control - especially the negative emotions, that listening to and acting upon emotions become **unhealthy**.  

Negative emotions can often cloud your judgement and disguises your true needs and potential.
* **Anxiety** and **Fear** makes you say and do things you don't really want to
* **Stress** makes you hurry up to finish things, leaving parts halfway or hastily done
* **Anger** makes you say and do things you'd never normally do, often at the expense of yourself or others
* **Jealousy** makes you compare and abstain from certain situations, views, or people, out of insecurity and spite generated by a certain perspective of said situation

These emotions can also combine and culminate in a smorgasbord of discomfort and suffering.  
Take FOMO as an example: *Jealousy from seeing your friends enjoying themselves without you, causes stress, then generates anxiety about yourself and how you are not part of it and how you actually want to be more part of it but you are trying to justify it by telling yourself you've been kind of tired lately and you're not really socially available alot of the time and you're not really the socializing type but you also very much are a social person sometimes maybe and then you kind of just get angry at yourself for how **stupid** you are to hinder yourself like this!*

...


However...

...no matter how big these emotions feels in the moment...

...thoughts and emotions are no more than reactions arising within you, coming and going like waves in the ocean...

## Finding Balance In Life
Balance can be described as being "in the middle of", being in the middle ground. This means, to know what balance is, you have to have seen both ends of the "spectrum" of experiences. You don't know what you will like or dislike in life, if you do not try and see.   
For you, this means that: If you do not explore yourself, you will not know where the boundaries are, thus, balance cannot be achieved.  
**Exploring yourself**, learning where your boundaries are, and also pushing them, allows you to shift your perspective on yourself.  

## Thinking Straight
How we think affects how we feel. Good and bad thoughts hold alot of power over you.  
You might experience bad thoughts that stun you - it feels like a wave of sadness is washing over you. But why?  

Unhelpful thoughts provide little value to you, and recognizing bad thoughts when they appear is a good approach. Recognize them, and brush them away, rather than letting them get a hold of you.

## Physical health
Staying active is another key part to a healthy mind. Being present, being outdoors, or both - experiencing the world, and absorbing sunlight. This is great for boosting awakeness and mood, and is also what you probably need the most the moments you really feel like **not** going outside.
> Personally, this is my best remedy for days of low mood. Changing your environment, being with nature and physically active.

>>>> **Further Reading** 📖  
Moderate to intense physical excercise is directly related to increased levels of **BDNF** (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor), which directly affect your mental health and metal toughness in a positive way.  
Low BDNF levels are heavily linked to mental health conditions like depression and anxiety,

### Resting well
Part of physical health is also resting well. Good resting means resting by actually relaxing your mind and your body - doing something you like, or something that calms you - like listening to your favorite music, reading or being creative. Consuming overstimulating "emotion rollercoaster"-esque content on social media is **not** a good way to rest - this leaves your body wired and your feelings numbed, often draining you more than relaxing you.

## Summary
Cultivating a healthy mind is a multifaceted challenge, often requiring shift in several views. The solution is not the same for everyone, and it is not easy. But wanting to change, and taking small steps towards is it a great start.  
And at the end of the day, maybe we aren't all as bad as we might think we are...
