# Photography
All the photos on guttespinat.no have been taken by me over the years. I am by no means a pro at photography, but I enjoy capturing interesting places that might often be overlooked. I edit and use many of these pictures as backgrounds for the banner at the top of the website page.

This is the first banner I made for Guttespinat.no. The banner is a a crop of this picture below, captured on a stormy autumn evening by Matre Hydroelectric Power Station near Bergen, Norway.
