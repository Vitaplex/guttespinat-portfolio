# Free 3D models for Source engine games
Hello  
I am offering a limited free 3D modeling service. If you or someone you know are making a map for any Source engine game (GMod, HL2, TF2 etc.) and need a custom model, or just want something very specific, funny or personal made into a game-ready model, free of charge, this is the place.

## Why?!
In these modern times, where everything has to be paid for or rented - where ownership is in many aspects a mere illusion. It seems everything that's done on this "contemporary internet" is done for some short -or longterm monetary gain, or promotion of some product or service.  

I would like to go back to the old, interpersonal basics, and provide my interest and service to the public, for the gain of the public, free of charge. The Norwegian word "dugnad" comes to mind. (A 3D-modeling "dugnad", if you will)  
This is also to gauge the interest and see the organic reach this website has, (if any) and of course also get some more practice with 3D-modeling. I will not be promoting this service anywhere, and I'll see if it manages to reach someone who needs it.  
If this catches on and people start sharing this, then naturally the reach will not be quite organic, but still human-to-human driven, which is also aligns with what I'm trying to achieve.

To see my other work, head to [Personal projects - 3D-modeling](https://guttespinat.no/#personalprojects3dmodeling)
